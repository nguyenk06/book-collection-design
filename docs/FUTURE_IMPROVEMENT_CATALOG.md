# Future Improvement Catalog

This is the Planner/Designer-owned catalog of product and UX opportunities learned from comparable applications. Entries are candidates, not roadmap commitments. Weekly rank is relative within this catalog and does not alter [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) priority.

## Workflow

- Planner and Designer review rank and disposition during weekly planning.
- `Promoted` means accepted into a named roadmap/workstream or brief; promotion must be recorded in the relevant authority.
- `Applied` requires verified implementation evidence. `Rejected` and `Superseded` remain concise to prevent rediscovery.
- Engineer feasibility belongs in the Engineer-local Research Sandbox defined by the [Knowledge Ownership Index](KNOWLEDGE_INDEX.md), not in this repository.
