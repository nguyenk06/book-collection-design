# Project Vision

## Purpose

Help a book collector understand, maintain, and complete a personal collection with less manual effort and greater confidence while shopping.

## Scope

- Maintain a reliable record of owned and wanted books.
- Support fast, book-first collection workflows.
- Identify likely matches from scans and imported data.
- Present the collection as a useful digital bookshelf.
- Improve incomplete or uncertain records through review and enrichment.
- Preserve portable data and recoverable assets.

## Non-goals

- General-purpose library circulation or patron management.
- Social networking, reviews, or public marketplace features.
- Perfect bibliographic cataloging before a book can enter the collection.
- Edition-level precision at the expense of completing the collection record.
- Replacing source data with unreviewed AI output.

## Design philosophy

- **Collector first:** Optimize for decisions collectors make while acquiring and organizing books.
- **Book first:** A useful book record can exist before every edition detail is known.
- **Completion over perfection:** Capture the collection broadly, then improve confidence and detail.
- **Evidence over assumptions:** Distinguish observed, imported, inferred, and reviewed information.
- **Human control:** Automation proposes; the collector confirms consequential changes.
- **Portable by default:** Core collection data must remain importable, exportable, and understandable.
- **Progressive enrichment:** Covers, metadata, tags, and edition detail enhance rather than block core workflows.

## Collector-first workflow

1. Capture or find a book quickly.
2. Determine whether it is owned, wanted, or uncertain.
3. Record the minimum information needed to act.
4. Preserve match evidence and confidence.
5. Review uncertain or incomplete records later.
6. Enrich presentation and metadata without disrupting the collection record.

## Current priorities

1. Verify the current database and establish a reliable baseline.
2. Make Shopping Mode the fastest path to an ownership decision.
3. Improve scanner matching and review of uncertain results.
4. Strengthen collection browsing, portability, and data quality.
