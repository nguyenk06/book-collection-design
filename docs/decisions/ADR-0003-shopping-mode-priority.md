# ADR-0003: Shopping Mode Is the Highest User Priority

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The most time-sensitive collector decision occurs while encountering a potential purchase. Slow lookup or unclear ownership state creates duplicates and missed opportunities.

## Decision

After the database review, Shopping Mode is the highest-priority user-facing workstream.

## Consequences

- Mobile speed, clarity, and scanner integration receive early attention.
- Supporting database and matching work should be evaluated against this workflow.
- Broader bookshelf and administrative improvements follow unless they unblock Shopping Mode.
- The priority may be superseded after the workflow reaches an agreed maturity level.
