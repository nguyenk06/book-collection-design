# Backlog

This document tracks unaccepted and exploratory ideas without assigning roadmap priority. Accepted work belongs in the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md); immediately actionable work belongs in [Next Actions](NEXT_ACTIONS.md).

## Candidate Ideas

No candidate ideas are currently recorded.

When adding an idea, use the next stable `BL-####` identifier:

| ID | Idea | Problem or opportunity | Evidence | Dependencies | Last reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BL-####` | Concise idea name | Why it may matter | Link or brief observation | Known prerequisites, if any | YYYY-MM-DD |

An entry records an idea, not approval, priority, or a commitment to implement it.

## Intake Rules

- Keep each idea brief and specific.
- State the problem before suggesting a solution.
- Link to existing documentation instead of repeating it.
- Record supporting evidence and known dependencies when available.
- Keep ideas within the scope defined by [Project Vision](PROJECT_VISION.md).
- Do not assign roadmap priority or implementation dates here.
- Follow the sanitization rules in [Documentation Rules](DOCUMENTATION_RULES.md).

## Promotion

Before an idea becomes accepted work:

1. Validate it against project scope and architecture principles.
2. Document relevant tradeoffs and dependencies.
3. Create an ADR if it establishes a major architectural decision.
4. Add it to the roadmap only with explicit product or architectural justification.
5. Remove it from Candidate Ideas and record the disposition below.
